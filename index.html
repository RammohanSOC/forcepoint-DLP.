Common questions

What does Forcepoint DLP do?

It helps protect sensitive information and reduce unauthorized data exposure by applying policies to how data is used and shared. No tool can guarantee that data will never leave, so we pair the technology with well-tuned policies and ongoing support.

Do you work with other data protection tools too?

Yes. We also work with AvePoint for data protection and management, and with Purview and DLP policies in Microsoft 365, so you can compare options before you decide.

Will DLP get in the way of my staff's work?

We roll out in stages and tune policies with your team, so legitimate work is interrupted as little as possible while sensitive data stays protected.

Can you manage DLP for us after deployment?

Yes. We offer ongoing support so your team does not have to maintain policies on its own. The exact scope is confirmed in a written proposal.

Can we buy or renew Forcepoint licenses through ISS?

Yes. We provide procurement and licensing services and help right-size licenses to your needs.

Will DLP make us compliant with GDPR or HIPAA?

No. DLP controls support your readiness and audits, but this is not a certification and does not guarantee compliance.
